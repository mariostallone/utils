Draw a house room by room, furnish it, plan the electrics, then look at it in 3D or walk through it.

Getting started

Open File and choose New blank plan, or New from the example home to see a finished house first. Every change saves in this browser as you go. Your plans are listed under File.

The bar at the top switches between three views:

  • Plan is where you draw and edit, seen from above.
  • 3D shows the house as a model you can turn around.
  • Walk puts you inside at eye height.

In the plan, drag empty space to move around and scroll or pinch to zoom. The ⤢ button fits the whole plan on screen. ft · in switches between feet and metres.

Draw rooms and walls

The quickest start is the Room tool: drag a rectangle and you get four walls and a room. Draw the next room against the first; shared walls join up.

For other shapes use the Wall tool:

  1. Click where the wall starts.
  2. Click at each corner. Walls snap to corners, to other walls, to the grid and to 15° angles. Hold Shift to draw at any angle.
  3. For an exact length, type it while drawing, such as 12'6" or 3.8m, and press Enter.
  4. Click your starting point, double-click, or press Esc to finish.

Rooms appear by themselves as soon as walls close a space. Click a room to name it, choose its floor and wall paint, or mark it as an outdoor space with no ceiling.

Use the presets in the panel for inside walls, outside walls and low garden walls before you draw.

Change walls

Pick the Select tool and click a wall. In the panel you can type its length, thickness and height.

  • Drag a blue dot to move a corner. Walls joined to it follow.
  • Drag the wall itself, or its orange square, to slide it. The room grows or shrinks with it.
  • Use this thickness everywhere applies the thickness to every full-height wall on the level.
  • Delete removes the selected wall.

Doors and windows

Pick the Door or Window tool, choose a type in the panel, then click on a wall. A red marker means the wall is too short or something is already there.

Click a door or window afterwards to change its width, height and sill height, or its distance from either corner. For doors you can swap the hinge side and the way the door opens. You can also drag it along the wall.

Furniture

Pick the Furniture tool, choose an item, and click where it goes. Press R to turn it before you place it. Items such as beds, sofas and counters snap flat against a wall.

Click a piece to change its size, colour and label. Drag it to move it, or drag the round handle to turn it. A red outline means a problem, such as overlapping another item, blocking a door, or too little room in front to use it. The panel says which.

Under Structure you find stairs, columns, radiators and floor openings.

Trace over a plan image

If you have a drawing or photo of a floor plan:

  1. Select nothing, then in the panel choose Choose an image.
  2. Click two points on the image that you know the distance between, such as both ends of a dimensioned wall, and type that distance.
  3. Drag the image into place and click Done.
  4. Draw walls on top of it with the Wall tool.

The slider in the panel makes the image stronger or fainter.

Levels and stairs

Use the level list in the top bar and choose Add a level. Copy the walls starts the new floor with the outside walls of the floor below, so you only draw the inside.

To connect floors, put Stairs on the lower level and a Floor opening for stairs in the same place on the upper level. The level below shows faintly in the plan so you can line things up.

Electrical plan

Pick the Electrical tool and a point type, then click next to a wall on the side where the point goes. Ceiling lights and fans go inside a room.

  • Click a point to set its height, circuit and a note for the electrician.
  • Link switch to light: click a switch, then the light it controls.
  • Open plan for electrician makes a numbered drawing with a legend and a list of every point. Positions are measured from the corners, the way an electrician measures on site. Save it as a PDF, an image or a spreadsheet list.

3D view and photos

Drag to turn the house, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag to slide. You can click furniture and drag it into place here too.

The panel lets you show the roof and the levels above, lower the walls to see inside, and pick the colour of the outside walls. Photo mode makes a realistic still: frame the shot, pick the time of day or an evening scene lit by your planned lights, and choose Render photo. It adds soft shadows, shading in corners and reflections, and renders at up to 3200 pixels wide. In Walk, the Photo button does the same from eye height.

Walk through and tours

In Walk, click the floor to walk there, or tap a spot on the small map. Drag to look around. On a computer, W A S D or the arrow keys move you; on a tablet, use the joystick.

Evening lights the house only with the lights in your electrical plan, so dark corners show up.

A tour is a guided walk you can replay:

  1. Open Tours and create one.
  2. Walk to a spot, face what you want to show, and choose Add this view as a stop. Add a caption.
  3. Repeat for each stop, then choose Play tour.
  4. Record video plays the tour and saves it as a video file you can send.

Saving and sharing

Plans save in this browser only. To keep a copy, or to move a plan to another computer or tablet, open File and choose Save plan file. On the other device, use Open a plan file.

If someone else opens this page, they see their own plans, not yours. Send them your plan file so they can open it. Undo (↶) and Redo (↷) cover every change.
